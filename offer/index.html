Kesyon Frekant

FAQ

Ou Gen Kesyon?

Have Questions?

Kòman mwen aksede pwogram nan apre mwen peye? How do I access my program after payment? +

Ou resevwa yon imèl ak enfòmasyon koneksyon ou nan 24 èdtan. Telechaje aplikasyon EP Akademi sou iOS oswa Android, konekte, epi tout kontni pwogram ou ap disponib imedyatman.

You'll receive a login email within 24 hours. Download the EP Akademi app on iOS or Android, log in, and all your program content will be immediately available.

Eske mwen bezwen eksperyans pou kòmanse? Do I need prior experience? +

Non — tout pwogram nou yo fèt pou debutan konplè. Nou kòmanse depi baz la epi pwogrese etap pa etap.

No — all our programs are built for complete beginners. We start from the basics and progress step by step.

Eske mwen ka enskri nan plis pase yon pwogram? Can I enroll in more than one program? +

Wi — ou ka enskri nan plizyè pwogram ak menm kont EP Akademi ou a. Tout kontni ou ap nan app la, òganize pa pwogram.

Yes — you can enroll in multiple programs with the same EP Akademi account. All your content will be in the app, organized by program.

Kisa garanti 7 jou a vle di? What does the 7-day guarantee mean? +

Si ou achte epi ou pa satisfè pou nenpòt rezon nan 7 premye jou yo, voye nou yon imèl epi nou va ranbouse ou 100% — san kesyon, san atant.

If you purchase and are unsatisfied for any reason within the first 7 days, email us and we'll refund you 100% — no questions asked, no waiting period.

Ki lang pwogram yo ye? What language are the programs in? +

Tout pwogram nou yo ansèye nan Kreyòl Ayisyen — pou Ayisyen yo ka aprann biznis anliy nan lang yo konprann pi byen an.

All programs are taught in Haitian Creole — so Haitian entrepreneurs can learn online business in the language they understand best.
